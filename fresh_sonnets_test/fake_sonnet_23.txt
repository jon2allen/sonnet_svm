Upon the Breath of Fleeting Hours We Tread

O fleeting breath of time, that steals so sly,
The rose’s blush, the lark’s melodious tune,
The golden sun that climbs the vaulted sky,
And fades too soon beneath the pallid moon.

What hand hath wrought this cruel and cunning art,
To bid fair beauty wither in its prime?
The violet fades, the daisy falls apart,
Yet still the stars endure beyond their time.

But lo! The brook that winds through verdant glades,
Though time may bend its course with careless hand,
Still sings its song through ever-changing shades,
And mirrors heaven’s unchanging, bright command.

So too shall love outlive the fleeting day,
When time itself is lost to time’s decay.
