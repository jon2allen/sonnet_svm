When golden hours like petals drift away,
And time’s swift hand doth steal the rose’s hue,
What once was bright now fades in ashen gray,
Yet still the earth renews its ancient vow.

The brook that sang in silver-lighted tune
Now murmurs low where shadows softly creep,
The lark that soared at morning’s blush of June
Now folds her wings where dusk doth lull to sleep.

O fleeting grace! O beauty’s borrowed breath!
Like April’s bloom outworn by summer’s flame,
Thou dost but mock the quiet face of death,
And leave behind a whisper, not a name.

Yet in the hush where twilight bends to night,
Love’s ghost still lingers in the fading light.
