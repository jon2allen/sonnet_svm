Upon the breath of fleeting hours we tread,
Where golden dawns in crimson robes do fade,
And beauty, like the rose by time betrayed,
Doth wither ‘neath the sun’s unyielding thread.
O fleeting sprite! Why dost thou thus depart,
Leaving thy trace in petals soft and pale,
As though the wind, with wanton art’s avail,
Could steal the hue from nature’s tender heart?

The brook, once bright with laughter’s silver chime,
Now murmurs low of days forever gone,
While oak trees stand in silent, stately prime,
Their roots entwined with echoes of the dawn.
Yet mark how time, though cruel in his flight,
Doth weave all change to stars of endless light.
