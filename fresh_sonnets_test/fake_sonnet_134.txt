Upon the dial’s face where shadows creep,
And golden hours dissolve in silent flight,
The rose, once bold, now droops in fading light,
While time, that thief, doth steal both bloom and sleep.
O fleeting sprite! What hand may bid thee stay,
When silver streams carve valleys through the stone,
And youth, once bright as dawn’s unblemished ray,
Fades like the echo of a half-known tone?

Yet in the hush where twilight weaves her spell,
The earth still breathes in verdant, whispered rhyme,
And though the stars may pale and seasons swell,
Some ghost of beauty lingers past its prime.
For time may mar, yet cannot quite undo
The grace that lingers in the morning’s dew.
