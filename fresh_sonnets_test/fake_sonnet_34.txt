Upon the brow of Time, where seasons tread,
There blooms a rose whose crimson lips do fade,
As morning’s kiss upon the dew is laid,
And fleeting hours in silver whispers spread.
O cruelest thief! That steals the golden thread
Where youth and beauty weave their bright parade,
Yet leaves the soul in shadows, unafraid,
To walk where autumn’s melancholy’s led.

The brook that laughed in spring now murmurs low,
Its voice grown hoarse with tales of love’s deceit,
While willows bend to kiss the stream below,
And sigh for days they’ll nevermore repeat.
Yet in this hush, where time and nature meet,
A fleeting grace in every leaf we know.
