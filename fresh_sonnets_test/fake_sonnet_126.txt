Upon the Breath of Fleeting Moments’ Flight

O golden hour, too swift in haste to stay,
Thy gilded beams do kiss the verdant plain,
Yet lo! before the dusk may bid thee nay,
Thou steal’st away like whispers in the rain.
The rose, once fair, now droops her weary head,
Her crimson cheek by time’s unkind hand pressed,
And though her scent still lingers where she’s led,
Her bloom must fade, her beauty unconfessed.

The brook that sings in silver-throated tune,
Its murmurs woven through the willow’s shade,
Shall cease ere long beneath the waning moon,
Its voice outworn, its melody decayed.
Yet in the hush where time and nature meet,
Beauty is but the echo of thy feet.
