When golden hours like petals softly fall,
And time’s swift hand doth steal the rose’s hue,
What once was bright now fades beyond recall,
As fleeting dawn yields to the evening’s dew.
The brook that laughed in silver, clear, and free,
Now murmurs low beneath the willow’s shade,
While youth, once bold, doth bend the willing knee,
And beauty’s reign must yield to time’s parade.

Yet in the hush where twilight’s shadows creep,
A whispered song the ancient oaks repeat—
The earth remembers what the stars still keep,
And love, though dimmed, in memory stays sweet.
So let the seasons turn, the years unfold,
For time may take, but leaves its tale retold.
