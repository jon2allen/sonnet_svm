When golden hours like petals softly fall,
And time’s swift hand doth steal the rose’s hue,
What once was bright now fades beyond recall,
As fleeting as the morning’s tender dew.
The brook that laughed in silver, clear, and free,
Now murmurs low of summers long since past,
While trees that whispered secrets unto me
Stand bare beneath the winter’s chilling blast.

Yet in this hush where beauty seems undone,
A seed takes root where none had dared to grow,
And though the sun may set ere day be done,
The stars shall wake where twilight lays its snow.
So take this truth, though time may bid thee part—
What dies in form lives ever in the heart.
