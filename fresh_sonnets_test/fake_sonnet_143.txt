Upon the brow of time where seasons tread,
There blooms a rose whose crimson lips do fade,
As morning’s blush gives way to evening’s shade,
And youth, once bright, must bow to age’s dread.

The silver brook that through the meadows led,
Now whispers secrets to the willow’s blade,
While golden hours in silent haste have strayed,
And left their footprints on the path they sped.

O fleeting grace! O beauty’s borrowed light!
The lark still sings, though dawn no more is thine,
And stars that watched thee in thy splendor bright
Now gaze upon a world less half divine.

Yet in the hush where time and nature meet,
Love’s ghost still walks on memory’s quiet feet.
