When golden hours like petals softly fall,
And time’s swift hand doth steal the rose’s hue,
What once was bright now fades beyond recall,
As fleeting dawn must yield to evening’s dew.
The brook that laughed in silver, clear, and free,
Now murmurs low with age’s weighty sigh,
While youthful blooms, once bold in revelry,
Bow heads beneath the ever-turning sky.

Yet in this hush, where shadows softly creep,
A whispered grace the withered world bestows—
The oak that stands though storms its branches sweep,
Still cradles nests where springtime’s music flows.
So beauty fades, yet in its fleeting flight,
Lives on in echoes of the endless light.
