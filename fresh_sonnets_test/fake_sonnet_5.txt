Upon the Fleeting Breath of Golden Hours

O fleeting breath of golden-tinted days,
When time doth steal the rose from beauty’s cheek,
And silver threads in nature’s tresses play,
While youth’s bright fire doth softly, slowly leak.
The lark, that once did greet the dawn’s first light,
Now falters ‘neath the weight of evening’s sigh,
And streams that laughed in crystal, pure delight,
Now murmur low beneath a leaden sky.

Yet still the earth, in robes of green arrayed,
Doth mock our grief with blossoms fresh and fair,
As if to say that time, though unafraid,
May spare some grace to linger in the air.
So take this hour, this bloom, this fleeting spark,
For time will turn all gold to shadow dark.
