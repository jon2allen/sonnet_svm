Upon the dial’s face where shadows creep,
And golden hours in silent haste do glide,
The rose, once flushed with youth’s unblushing pride,
Now droops her head where time doth secrets keep.
O fleeting sprite! That in the meadows wide
Didst dance with zephyrs light and shadows deep,
Now dust thou art, yet in thy slumber steeped,
The earth reclaims what once was thine to guide.

The brook that sang of love in murmurs clear,
Now whispers low to stones of ages past,
While silvered leaves in autumn’s breath appear,
A ghostly train of joys too fleet, too vast.
Yet in this change, where all things fade and sever,
Beauty endures—though time may wither never.
