Upon the Breath of Fleeting Moments Cast

O fleeting breath of time, that steals so sly,
The rose’s blush, the lark’s melodious song,
The golden hue of dawn that lights the sky,
And all that doth to nature’s realm belong.
Yet in thy haste, thou dost not spare the grace
Of beauty’s form, nor stay thy ruthless hand,
But bear’st away each fair and fleeting face,
As waves erase the prints upon the sand.

Yet lo! the earth, though stript of summer’s pride,
Doth clothe herself in robes of frost and snow,
And though the bloom of youth may fade and glide,
The soul doth ripen with the years that flow.
So let me drink the draught of time’s own cup,
And find in change the constancy I sup.
