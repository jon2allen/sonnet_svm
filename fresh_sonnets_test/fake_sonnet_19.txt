When golden hours like petals softly fall,
And time’s swift hand doth steal the rose’s hue,
What once was bright now fades beyond recall,
Yet in its wake doth leave a ghostly dew.

The brook that sang in silver-throated cheer,
Now murmurs low where willows bend and weep,
While seasons dance in cycles ever near,
And beauty sleeps in earth’s unyielding keep.

O fleeting bloom! O light too soon undone!
The lark that soared at dawn now folds her wing,
And twilight’s hush claims all beneath the sun,
Save echoes where the vanished moments cling.

Yet in the dust where time his trophies lays,
Love’s breath revives what death in silence stays.
