Upon the breath of fleeting hours we tread,
Where golden dawns in crimson robes do fade,
And beauty, like the rose in summer’s bed,
Doth bow her head ere twilight’s shade is laid.

O fleeting sprite! That dost in blossoms dwell,
Thy laughter rings where silver brooks do glide,
Yet time, that reaps with unrelenting swell,
Doth steal thee hence to silence and the tide.

The lark that soars at morning’s pearled gate,
The violet hid where mossy stones entwine,
The star that trembles at the night’s first state—
All yield unto the scythe of hours divine.

Yet in the heart, where love and memory twine,
Thy ghostly hue doth evermore abide.
