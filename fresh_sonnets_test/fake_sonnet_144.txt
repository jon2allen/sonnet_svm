Upon the dial’s face where shadows creep,
The golden hours do steal with silent tread,
And beauty fades as blossoms bow their head,
While time, the thief, doth secrets dark and deep.
The rose, once bright, now droops in slumber’s keep,
Its crimson hue to ashen grey is fled,
And summer’s song in winter’s chill is dead,
Yet memory doth in twilight vigils weep.

O fleeting world! Where youth and joy take flight,
Like swallows swift that vanish with the sun,
Leaving but echoes in the hollow night.
Yet mark how dawn still breaks when day is done,
For though all else may fade and turn to dust,
Love’s light endures beyond the reach of rust.
