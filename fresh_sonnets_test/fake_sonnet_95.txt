Upon the Breath of Fleeting Hours We Tread

O fleeting breath of time, that steals so sly,
The rose’s blush, the lily’s snowy hue,
And leaves but dust where golden moments flew,
As stars must fade beneath the morning’s eye.
The brook that laughed in silver melody,
Now whispers low to banks it once knew true,
And youth, once bright as dawn in heaven’s view,
Doth wither slow beneath the autumn sky.

Yet mark thee well, though beauty fades to clay,
And nature’s hand doth paint in hues of gray,
The soul that loved her light shall never part.
For time may mar the form, yet not the art—
The echo lingers in the heart’s own lay,
And love outlives the shadow of decay.
