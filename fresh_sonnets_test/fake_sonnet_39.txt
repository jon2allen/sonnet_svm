Upon the fleeting breath of golden hours,
Where time doth weave his tapestry so bright,
The rose, once proud in summer’s sweetest powers,
Now bows her head to autumn’s fading light.
O cruelest thief! That steal’st with silent tread,
The bloom from cheeks where laughter once did play,
And leaves the streams where youthful footsteps sped,
To murmur low of joys that passed away.

Yet in the hush of twilight’s soft embrace,
When silver stars awake to claim the sky,
The earth doth whisper secrets to her face,
And beauty lingers, though we know not why.
For though the petal falls and time decays,
Love’s echo blooms in everlasting rays.
