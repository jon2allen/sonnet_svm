When golden hours like petals drift away,
And time’s unkind hand steals the rose’s hue,
What once was bright now fades to ashen grey,
Yet still the earth renews its verdant view.
The brook that sang in silver-throated glee
Now murmurs low where willows bend and weep,
While summer’s blaze yields to the frost’s decree,
And beauty sleeps in winter’s hollow keep.

O fleeting bloom! O moment swift and sweet,
Why dost thou mock the heart with brief delight?
The lark may soar, the violet kiss the feet,
But both must bow to time’s relentless flight.
Yet in the hush where twilight shadows creep,
Love’s ghost still walks where nature’s secrets sleep.
