When golden hours like petals softly fall,
And time’s swift hand doth steal the rose’s hue,
The brook still hums, though youth’s bright fire is through,
And winter’s breath doth kiss the fading wall.

O fleeting bloom! O sun that will not stay!
The lark ascends, yet downward sinks the star,
The oak outlives the violet by a year,
While beauty fades like dew at break of day.

Yet in the hush where twilight weaves her spell,
The earth remembers what the sky forgets—
The whispered vow, the echo none repeals,
The root that clings when summer’s crown is set.

So take this hour, though time its debt may claim,
For love’s the spark that outlasts beauty’s flame.
