Upon the brow of Time, where seasons play,
There blooms a rose whose hue outshines the sun,
Yet lo! the wind doth steal its sweet away,
And leaves but thorns where once soft petals run.
The brook that sang in silver, clear, and bright,
Now murmurs low beneath the weight of years,
Its voice grown faint, its sparkle dimmed by night,
A ghost of joy that once dispelled all fears.
O fleeting grace! O beauty’s fragile reign!
The golden hour doth slip like sand through glass,
And all the earth must bear the frost’s cold chain,
When summer’s fire to embers doth pass.
Yet in the heart, where memory doth dwell,
The rose still blooms, the brook sings clear and well.
