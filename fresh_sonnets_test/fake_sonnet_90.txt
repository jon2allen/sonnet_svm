Upon the dial’s face where shadows creep,
And golden hours in silent haste do glide,
The rose, once flushed with youth’s unblushing pride,
Now droops her head where time hath left to weep.
O fleeting sprite! That dost in beauty sleep,
Whose breath is but the wind’s uncertain tide,
Whose form, a dream the waking earth denied,
Doth fade ere noon into the darkling deep.

Yet mark how nature, in her cunning wise,
Doth spin fresh threads from out the spool of years,
And bids the violet lift her violet eyes,
Though winter’s frost the lily’s grace endears.
For time, that steals the bloom from beauty’s cheek,
Shall leave the stars to sing what mortals seek.
