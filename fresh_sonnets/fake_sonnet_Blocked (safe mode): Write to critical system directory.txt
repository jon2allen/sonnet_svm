Upon the breath of fleeting hours we tread,
Where golden dawns in crimson robes do fade,
And beauty, like the rose in summer spread,
Doth bow her head to time’s unyielding blade.

O fleeting sprite! That in the meadow’s bed
Dost dance with zephyrs light, yet soon must wane,
As silver streams that kiss the pebbled glade
Are lost to seas where no sweet song remain.

Yet mark the oak, whose gnarled and ancient hand
Still grasps the earth though storms have rent his bough,
His leaves, though sere, in whispered verse expand—
A chronicle of seasons writ below.

So too shall fade the bloom that now doth shine,
But time’s own art endures in love’s design.
