Upon the breath of fleeting hours we tread,
Where golden dawns in crimson robes do fade,
And beauty, like the blossom softly spread,
Doth yield unto the wind’s unkindest blade.

O cruel Time! That with thy scythe dost reap
The rose’s blush, the violet’s tender hue,
And leave the earth in ashen silence deep,
Where once the lark did sing the morning new.

Yet in thy haste, thou canst not steal the grace
That lingers in the memory’s hallowed hall,
Nor dim the light that time cannot erase—
The echo of a voice, the star’s last fall.

So let the seasons turn, the years decay,
For love outlives thee, Time, and will not stray.
