Upon the fleeting breath of golden hours,
Where Phoebus’ steeds do chase the silver night,
The blooming meads in verdant vestments dight,
Do whisper secrets to the woodland bowers.
O fleeting span! O transitory powers,
That steal the rose from youth’s untainted sight,
And leave but dust where once did shine so bright
The lily’s grace, the violet’s sweet dow’rs.

Yet mark thee well, though Time with ruthless hand
Doth pluck the blossoms from the springing year,
And bid the sun his radiant course command,
The earth, fair dame, doth still her charms revere.
For though the leaf doth fade, the branch doth stand,
And Nature’s love outlives the fleeting sphere.
