**Sonnet of the Turning Glass**

O cruelest glass where time doth show his art,
That turns the ruby lip to pallid snow,
And steals the fire from beauty's burning heart,
As winter's breath doth bid the green things go.

The lily, once in ivory pride arrayed,
Now bows her head where frost hath kissed the ground,
The skylark's song in hollow echoes fades,
And silence wraps the woods in frozen sound.

Yet mark how silver threads the black boughs trace,
And paint the hills with alabaster light,
The moon that walks in solitary grace,
And stars that watch the dying of the night.

So fades the rose, so fleets the fleeting breath,
Yet time's own hand doth weave them into death.
