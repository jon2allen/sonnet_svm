**Sonnet of the Shifting Hours**

O cruelest glass where Time doth show his might,
That turns the rose's blush to ashen gray,
And steals the fire from Beauty's burning light,
As winter's breath doth bid the green things stray.

The lily, once in ivory pride arrayed,
Now bows her head where frost hath kissed the ground,
The skylark's song in hollow echoes fades,
And silence wraps the woods in frozen sound.

Yet mark how silver threads the black bough's lace,
And paints the hills with alabaster light,
The moon that walks in solitary grace,
And stars that watch the dying of the night.

So fades the bloom, so fleets the fleeting breath,
Yet Time's own hand doth weave them into death.
