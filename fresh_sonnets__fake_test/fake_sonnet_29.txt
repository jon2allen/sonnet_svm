**Sonnet of the Turning Glass**

When April's blush hath kissed the waking rose,
And summer's gold doth melt to autumn's gray,
What subtle hand unseen the bloom bestows,
Then steals the light of beauty's bright array?

The lily, once in ivory pride displayed,
Now bows her head where winter's breath doth creep,
The nightingale that sang the livelong day
Now silent sits where frost hath sealed her sleep.

Yet mark how silver decks the barren bough,
And paints the mead with hues of ghostly white,
The moon that walks the star-strewn heavens now,
And stars that gem the sable robe of night.

So fades the bloom, so fleets the fleeting hour,
Yet time's own glass doth guard their fading power.
