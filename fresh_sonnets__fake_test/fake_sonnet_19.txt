**Sonnet of the Fleeting Span**

When April's blush hath kissed the waking rose,
And summer's gold doth yield to autumn's gray,
What hand unseen doth bid the blossom close,
And steal the light of beauty's bright array?

The violet, once in purple pride arrayed,
Now bows her head where winter's breath doth creep,
The lark that soared in morning's golden shade
Now silent lies where frost hath sealed her sleep.

Yet lo! the rime that decks the barren bough,
And paints the mead with hues of silver bright,
The moon that walks the star-strewn heavens now,
And stars that gem the sable robe of night.

So fades the bloom, so fleets the fleeting day,
Yet Time's harsh hand doth bear them all away.
