**Sonnet of the Plowman’s Surety**

The oxen low where furrowed writs are sown,
Their yokes like bonds that drag the share’s decree—
No charter grants the clod its right to groan,
Yet still the soil must answer plowman’s fee.

What mariner steers by the harrow’s trace,
Or plots his course where stubble strews the lea?
No astrolabe can fix this shifting place,
Nor sextant mark what th’ earth will yield to me.

The scythe, once bright as any baron’s blade,
Now dulls where weeds the harvest’s law defy;
The granary, sworn to keep the tithe it made,
Doth gape with chaff where no true grain doth lie.

Yet still I till, though seed be ne’er repaid—
For love’s the crop no drought can e’er invade.
