**Sonnet of the Shipwright’s Demurrer**

The admiralty’s writ may stay the tide,
Yet not the oak that bends beneath thy hand—
No notary’s seal could make the keel withstand
The gale that blows where thou dost bid it glide.

The mariner’s astrolabe, though bright,
Must yield to thine unerring sextant’s art;
The compass spins, yet thou dost play the part
Of lodestar fixed where no true north is sighted.

What herald’s blazon could thy worth impart,
When even waves their ancient writ deny?
The caulker’s pitch, though black as usury,
Must fail where thou dost drive the adze’s smart.

Then let the courts their parchment pleas defer—
I’ll build my case in timber thou dost steer.
