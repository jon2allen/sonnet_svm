**Sonnet of the Navigator’s Quitclaim**

The lead-line sings where hidden shoals conspire,
A knotted tongue that warns the helm’s decree—
No admiral’s seal can quench the deep’s dark fire,
Nor writ of marque command what tides decree.

What husbandman would sow his seed in brine,
Or trust the furrow to the salt’s harsh lease?
No plowshare parts the waves’ unyielding line,
Nor harrow breaks what Neptune holds in fee.

The lodestone, once true as a herald’s crest,
Now veers where no man’s compass dares to go;
The chart, once clear, is blurred by time’s unrest,
And stars grow dim where no true course may show.

Yet still I sail, though haven ne’er be found—
For love’s the port no storm can drown or bound.
