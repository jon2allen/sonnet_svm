**Sonnet of the Mariner’s Indenture**

Where leaden lines descend to fathomless dark,
Thy name is writ in currents’ covenant—
No charter’s wax nor admiralty’s grant
Could bind the tide that bears thee through the mark.

The lodestone falters, drawn to thine own pole,
And sextant’s arc must yield to truer guide;
The leeward shore, though charted, slips aside
When thou dost steer the helm with unseen tiller’s toll.

What husbandman could reap such salted yield,
Or plowman turn the furrow of thy wake?
The barnacle’s grip, though stubborn, soon must break,
Yet thou dost cleave the deep with keel unsealed.

Then let the stars their ancient writ revoke—
I’ll sign my course in ink thy waves invoke.
