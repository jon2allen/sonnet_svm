**Sonnet of the Alchemist’s Ledger**

What crucible could hold thy shifting flame,
When mercury itself would flee the test?
No athanor’s heat nor philosopher’s quest
Can fix thee in the dross of common name.

The alembic drips its slow and measured gold,
Yet thou dost spill thy essence unconfined—
A quintessence no balance sheet may bind,
Nor notary’s seal make fast or sold.

The herald’s beast on thy device would starve,
For no blazon could bear thy restless hue;
The plowman’s furrow turns to barren dew
Where thou dost walk, and all his toil unnerve.

Then take this ink—let no man counter-sign
The debt I owe to thine unreckoned shine.
