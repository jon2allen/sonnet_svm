**Sonnet of the Tiller’s Audit**

O let me not thy furrowed trust enfeoff
To th’ idle plow that ne’er hath known the rein,
Nor yield thy lease of clods to winds aloof,
Who mock the seed with barren, salted rain.

The mariner who steers by stars untrue
Shall founder where the leeward rocks abound,
Yet thou dost chart by th’ compass of my vow—
A needle fixed, though tempests howl around.

What alchemist could coax from leaden ore
A grain so bright as mercy in thy glance?
The herald’s blazon fades, the law’s writ bore
Its teeth to dust, yet still thy love doth dance.

Then take this yield, though scant the harvest be—
Th’ earth’s last audit pays in thee.
