**Sonnet of the Mariner’s Ledger**

O thou whose gaze doth chart my tides unseen,
As pilot marks the shoal with wary lead,
What writ of love is graved in wax so green
It binds my course though winds have ta’en the thread?

The audit of thy sighs, a debt unpaid,
Doth weigh my keel where barnacles adhere—
No lease of joy, but mortgage deep-laid,
Whose interest swells with each unspoken fear.

Yet lo! The alchemist’s retort doth hold
Thy breath distilled to gold beyond assay,
Though salt corrodes the compass I was told
To steer by stars that fade in break of day.

Thus enfeoff me to th’ uncharted deep,
Where love’s true north lies anchored—though I weep.
