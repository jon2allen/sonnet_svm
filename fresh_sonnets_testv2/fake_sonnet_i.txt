**Sonnet of the Mariner’s Ledger**

O thou whose gaze doth chart the tides’ deceit,
Where stars, like writs, in waxen heavens stand,
I’ve ta’en thy name in fealty complete—
A bond no storm nor audit may command.

The compass spins, yet ne’er my course doth stray,
Though currents drag the keel through briny deep;
Thy voice, a lodestar, bids the winds obey,
And stills the waves that else would wake my sleep.

Yet mark thee well—this lease on time is brief,
As quicksilver that slips from careless hands;
The ledger’s ink shall fade to ashen grief,
When winter’s quill the final sum demands.

So let the chart be signed in crimson tide,
Ere time’s high court our reckoning decide.
