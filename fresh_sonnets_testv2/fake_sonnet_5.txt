**Sonnet of the Tiller’s Audit**

O let me not thy furrowed lease enfeoff
To th’ wanton wind that scatters seed unspent—
The plowshare’s oath, though ne’er by parchment penned,
Doth bind thee closer than the scrivener’s proof.

Yet lo, the tide that drags the mariner’s chart
Through shoals where no astrolabe dares to pry,
Steals also th’ hourglass from the reeve’s wary eye,
And leaves the audit of our days in part.

What alchemist could cozen time’s assay,
Or turn the dross of toil to herald’s hue?
The scythe, not scepter, marks the season’s due,
And every stalk must yield its tithe of hay.

So take my hand—let no man countermand
The covenant writ deep in th’ earth’s own hand.
