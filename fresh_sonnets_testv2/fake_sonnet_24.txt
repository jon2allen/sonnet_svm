**Sonnet of the Alchemist’s Crucible**

Dost thou not see how base the dross appears
When molten lead is ta’en from furnace bright?
No sophic fire nor philosopher’s years
Can purge the slag that clings to mortal sight.

The athanor’s breath, though steady, cannot hold
The volatile that flees the alembic’s grasp—
Yet thou dost fix what time and change make cold,
And turn to gold the dross of sorrow’s clasp.

What writ could bind the transmutation’s art
That makes the vile to virtue’s height ascend?
The herald’s crest, the lawyer’s seal impart
No power when love’s last alchemy doth blend.

Then take this crucible, though cracked and worn—
The elixir’s truth is love’s unbroken morn.
