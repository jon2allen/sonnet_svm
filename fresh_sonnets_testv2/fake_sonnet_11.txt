**Sonnet of the Tilled Ledger**

O let me not thy furrowed lease prolong,
Where th’ earth, enfeoffed to the plough’s decree,
Doth yield her tithe in clods of stubborn song—
Not grain, but writs of barren alchemy.

The mariner, who charts by starry audit,
Ne’er found a course so fraught with shifting sand
As I, who till where no man’s mark hath stood it,
Yet bind my toil in contracts writ by hand.

What herald’s blazon shines upon my spade?
No crest, but rust that doth the iron claim,
No gilded charge, but worms that ply their trade
Where once I turned the soil to bear my name.

Yet still I delve, though harvest ne’er be ta’en—
For love’s the debt no audit can sustain.
