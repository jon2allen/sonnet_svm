**Sonnet of the Tiller’s Audit**

O let me not thy furrowed lease prolong,
Nor bind thy ploughshare to th’ unyielding stone,
Where winter’s writ hath sealed the soil’s wrong,
And barren sward doth mock the seed outgrown.

Yet mark how yonder marl, by spade laid bare,
Doth counterfeit the ledger’s honest sum—
A debt of green, though ne’er accounted there,
Where root and rain conspire to strike the drum.

What alchemist could turn this clod to gold,
Or chart the tide that lifts the buried grain?
No herald’s blazon shines on furrow old,
Nor compass points where hidden streams remain.

Then take this audit, love—let fallow lie,
And trust the earth to reckon by the sky.
